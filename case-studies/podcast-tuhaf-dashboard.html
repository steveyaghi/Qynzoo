The Problem

Marketing took the podcast's founders many hours for every episode. They needed to see how posts performed across platforms, but more than that, they needed something that understood who they were trying to reach and could help grow the audience for YouTube videos, Shorts and Reels, not just report numbers.

The Approach

We built a dashboard connected to every platform the podcast posts on, with an AI strategist on top. It analyses posting times and reach, reads the content of each episode, and advises on the whole workflow, from the script to the finished video, including which clips have a strong enough hook to pull in new listeners.

Results

The founders now save about 2 days of work per episode and spend that time researching what to improve, based on the dashboards and the AI strategist's advice.
