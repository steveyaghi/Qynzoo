The Problem

As one of the largest retail networks in the Netherlands, Odido relies on detailed weekly reports pulled from several data sources. Many of these reports were built by hand every week and took up to 2 days to deliver. Manual work also meant errors and delays whenever something went wrong.

The Approach

We automated the full reporting process: Python and SQL collect and prepare the data, and Power BI and ThoughtSpot present and deliver the reports. No manual steps remain between pulling the data and sending the final report.

Python SQL PowerBI ThoughtSpot

Results

All reports now arrive every Monday between 9 and 10 am, with the same output every time. The team gets about 2 days a week back for actual strategy work instead of repeated tasks, and the manual-entry errors are gone.
