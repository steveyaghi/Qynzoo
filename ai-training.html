WHAT YOUR TEAM LEARNS

Practical skills they use the next day

Ask better questions

How to give AI the right context, an example and a clear goal, so the first answer is already usable.

Use it on real work

We practise on your own tasks: emails, reports, meeting notes, quotes and social media posts. No made-up examples.

Check what comes back

How to spot mistakes and invented facts, and when a person should always look before something goes out.

Pick the right tool

Which tool fits which job, so your team stops trying to do everything in one chat window.

Work safely with data

Keep what works

The team leaves with a shared prompt library for its own recurring tasks, so good results are easy to repeat.

THE EU AI ACT

The law asks for this too

Since 2 February 2025, the EU AI Act asks every company that uses AI to make sure its staff know enough about it. This is called AI literacy. A practical training on the tools your team really uses is a good way to take care of that.
