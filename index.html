04. ABOUT

Hi, I'm Mostafa.

I'm the founder of Qynzoo, based in the Netherlands. I build AI automation systems, machine learning models, and websites for companies that need real, working solutions, not another pitch deck about "AI transformation."

I hold a Master's in Civil Engineering and Management from the University of Twente, and my background is in applying machine learning to real engineering problems. Predicting soil behavior for dike stability at Fugro is one example. That engineering grounding is why I start from the actual data and the actual workflow on every project, not a generic template.

Every project on this site (Fugro, Haskoning, ODIDO, Waterprof) was built end-to-end by me, for a real team, solving a real bottleneck. If you'd rather talk to the person who'll actually build your solution than a sales team, that's exactly how I work.
