# Qynzoo copy rewrite (v5) — English draft for approval

Goal: Qynzoo as a Dutch B2B automation company for MKB, not a one-person
portfolio. Everything below is a proposal; nothing on the site has changed yet.
Once approved, this file is also the source for the Dutch translation.

## Voice rules

- **We / our** = Qynzoo. **You / your team** = the client.
- **I** only where a person signs: blog posts, and the founder quote on the About page.
- Plain words. No "engagement", "end-to-end", "seamless", "stunning", "leverage".
  Technical names (RAG, ML) only in the small "Under the hood" line.
- Every section points back to the three promises:
  **Save time. Fewer mistakes. Delivered on time.**
- No claims without a number from a real project. "We" never implies a big team.

2. **We copy data between systems** *(kept)*

   - Under the hood: Integration
3. **Every new project starts from a blank page** *(new)*
   - What we build: A system that writes the first draft from your past projects and your own templates.

4. **Writing the same kind of content takes hours** *(new, replaces "Social media eats my week")*

   - Under the hood: AI content assistant · *Proof: Waterprof*
5. **Nobody can find anything in our documents** *(kept)*
   - What we build: An assistant that reads your company files and answers questions, with a link to the source.
   - What you get: Answers in seconds instead of asking a colleague or digging through folders.
   - Under the hood: Document assistant (RAG)
6. **We want to see problems before they happen** *(kept, simplified)*
   - What we build: A model trained on your own data that flags what is likely next: demand, delays or maintenance.
   - What you get: Decisions based on a forecast, not a gut feeling.
