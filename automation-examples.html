WHAT TO AUTOMATE FIRST

How to tell if a process is worth automating

Ask these five questions about a task. As a rule of thumb, if you answer yes to three or more, it is probably worth a call.

  • It happens every day or every week.
  • It follows the same steps every time.
  • People copy or retype the same data.
  • Someone has to wait for it before they can continue.
  • A mistake in it costs time or money.

WHAT TO LEAVE ALONE

Not everything should be automated

Work that changes every time, needs judgement about people, or happens only a few times a year is usually not worth automating. We tell you honestly when software or a simple fix is enough.
