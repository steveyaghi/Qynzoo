ABOUT QYNZOO

We help teams stop doing the same work twice.

Qynzoo is a Dutch business automation company. We automate reports, data entry and documents so your team can focus on the work that needs people.

OUR PROMISE

Three things every project delivers

01

Save time

Your team stops doing the same task twice and spends the hours on the real work.

02

Fewer mistakes

No more copying between systems or retyping numbers. The system does it the same way every time.

03

Delivered on time

A clear scope, a fixed timeline and a working system. Most projects go live in 1 to 3 weeks.

THE FOUNDER

Mostafa Yaghi

"I'm Mostafa Yaghi, founder of Qynzoo. I studied Civil Engineering and Management at the University of Twente and started out applying machine learning to real engineering problems, like predicting soil behaviour for dike stability at Fugro."

"That background is why we always start from the actual data and the actual workflow, not a template. Before we build anything, we want to understand where your team loses time and where mistakes creep in."

Since then, Qynzoo has delivered automation for Haskoning, ODIDO and Waterprof: reports that arrive on time, drafts that save hours, and fewer manual steps.
